# Personal reading compass

Use for an explicit request to apply the user's reading, or an engineering/product decision where the reading map offers a concrete relevant lens. Routine edits do not need a book lookup.

Resolve `uadeVault` from `~/.agents/local-paths.json` through the existing `obsidian-vault` skill. The private entry point is `Efforts/Proyectos/Sistema de Agentes/Reading Compass.md`; technical sections are in `Technical Reading Notes.md` beside it. The complete `Reading Library Index.md` is for title/status lookup only. Do not preload the whole index, book collection or vault. Search the decision map/headings, then read only the relevant section and its necessary sources.

- Treat these as optional reasoning aids. Project code, approved requirements and current evidence take precedence; neither a book nor a shelf entry authorizes extra features, abstractions or workflow gates.
- Distinguish a source-supported idea, the agent's proposed application, the user's interpretation and an observed experiment. Do not invent the user's beliefs, ratings, chapter/page references or reading completion.
- A dated local snapshot is not live Goodreads verification. `read`, `reading`, `pending` and unknown stay separate; possession/downloads do not prove completion. Prefer a newer verified export when provided; preserve provenance and explain discrepancies.
- Explain at most one useful connection when it affects the decision. Literary themes are discussion prompts, not technical evidence, diagnosis or business guarantees. Technical examples remain illustrative until checked against the active code.
- Keep the personal catalog, class links, reviews and annotations in the private vault. Do not copy them to a public repository, worker prompt or external service without the relevant authorization. Give workers only the minimum non-sensitive principle needed for their scoped task.

If the vault or note is absent on another PC, use ordinary project guidance and report the missing source only when relevant. Do not create a replacement vault or infer that missing notes remove capabilities. This rule installs no MCP, indexer, vector store or new skill.
