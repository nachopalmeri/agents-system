# Product Foundry

## Goal

Help create many small product bets, validate them quickly, and scale only the ones with real market signals.

## Mental model

- Launch 15-20 small products before expecting a winner.
- Treat each product as a fishing rod in the market.
- Launch every 1-2 weeks when exploring.
- MVP means earliest testable/useful/lovable version.
- Follow money flows.
- Build for people already spending money or wasting time.
- Prefer existing behavior over behavior change.

## Idea lenses

1. ProductHunt, TrustMRR, Indie Hackers, Starter Story, X, Reddit.
2. AI upgrade of existing validated tools.
3. Simplify big software for a niche.
4. Acquisition, conversion, retention bottlenecks.
5. WhatsApp/email/Google Sheets/Notion workflows.
6. Internal tools turned into products.
7. Buyers with budget.
8. Agent-facing APIs/tools/docs.
9. AI copilots for existing roles.
10. Bad workarounds: copy-paste, messy spreadsheets, five tools glued together.

## Idea scorecard

Score 1-5:

- Existing money flow.
- Pain intensity.
- Buyer clarity.
- Reachability.
- MVP simplicity.
- Distribution fit.
- AI leverage.
- Retention potential.
- Founder fit.

## MVP patineta checklist

- One user.
- One painful job.
- One core workflow.
- One visible result.
- One CTA/payment/commitment.
- Manual backend allowed.
- Launchable in 1-2 weeks.

## Validation signals

Strong:

- Payment.
- Preorder.
- Repeated usage.
- Manual onboarding accepted.
- Users ask for more.
- Organic referrals.

Weak:

- Likes.
- Compliments.
- “Interesting idea”.
- Traffic with no conversion.
