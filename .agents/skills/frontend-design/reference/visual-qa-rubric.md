# Visual QA Rubric

Score each dimension 0, 1, or 2 from rendered screenshots and interaction evidence.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| subject specificity | interchangeable template | some subject cues | unmistakably tied to product/content |
| hierarchy | unclear action/content order | usable order | immediate, persuasive hierarchy |
| composition | accidental or repetitive | coherent | distinctive and controlled |
| typography | default or inconsistent | readable and consistent | expressive, justified, excellent rhythm |
| color rationale | decorative/inaccessible | functional roles | subject-linked roles with strong contrast |
| responsive integrity | broken or merely scaled | works at required widths | recomposes elegantly by context |
| accessibility | blocking keyboard/contrast/motion issue | baseline accessible | inclusive details improve the experience |
| motion/3D purpose | decorative or harmful | restrained and useful | clarifies hierarchy or deepens the subject |
| generic-pattern avoidance | obvious AI/SaaS template | some original choices | memorable signature without effect stacking |

Acceptance requires every dimension at least 1 and a total of at least 14/18. Evidence includes screenshots at 390x844 and 1440x900, plus 768x1024 when tablet composition changes; the task receipt lists defects and fixes.
